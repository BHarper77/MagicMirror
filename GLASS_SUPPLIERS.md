# Two-Way Mirror Glass — Supplier Research (Glasgow + UK)

Date: 2026-07-25. Frame is built and takes up to 6mm. **Glass only — no acrylic.**
Thinnest available preferred.

**Pane size: 412mm x 702mm** (0.29 m²). Note this is under the 0.5–1 m² minimum
charge most merchants apply to specialist glass, so expect flat minimums rather
than size-scaled quotes — the price gap between suppliers may be smaller than
you'd think.

## Headline findings

- **6mm is realistically the only thickness for two-way mirror _glass_ in the UK.**
  Pilkington **Mirropane™** — the standard product every UK supplier resells — is
  made in **6mm annealed only**, confirmed on Pilkington's own product page.
  Anything thinner in the two-way world is acrylic. A couple of sites hint at
  4mm two-way glass (see "4mm question" below) but nothing publishes it as a
  buyable option. Good news: your frame takes 6mm, so this is a non-issue.
- **Second pass on Glasgow found no merchant advertising two-way glass**, under
  any name — searched two-way, one-way, spy mirror, observation glass,
  surveillance glass, beamsplitter, TV mirror, Mirropane. Glasgow has _plenty_
  of custom-cut **mirror** merchants (Forsyth, D&N, Bearsden, Glass Age,
  Intaglio, Montrose, mirrors-glass.co.uk) — that's most likely what you
  remember. They cut silvered mirror, not beamsplitter.
- Pilkington lists only **four** UK Mirropane suppliers — Glass Design Solutions,
  Glass Services, Mackenzie Glass, Specialist Glass Products — **none in
  Scotland**. So there is no Scottish stockist in the official chain.
- **Still worth a Glasgow call.** Mirropane is ordinary annealed float glass to
  cut — any decent Glasgow glazier can order a lite and cut it. It's a "can you
  get this in for me" job, not a website order. Forsyth is the best bet.
- **Best online-orderable option: [Mirrors and Glass](https://www.mirrorsandglass.co.uk/shop/made-to-measure/two-way-mirrors)**
  — 6mm, full cut-to-size configurator, checkout online, MagicMirror-forum
  endorsed. Caveat: Scotland is **not** on their weekly delivery routes (see below).
- **Likely cheapest:** Mirrors and Glass, or a Glasgow glazier ordering a lite in
  (no freight on a large fragile pane, and you collect). Most expensive:
  twowaymirrors.com (US import) and UK Glass Centre (from £142.80/m² ex VAT,
  Cornwall, ~5–6 week lead time).

> Hello,
>
> I'm building a smart mirror and I'm looking for a single pane of two-way
> mirror glass (sometimes sold as one-way mirror, spy glass, or Pilkington
> Mirropane), cut to size.
>
> A few questions:
>
> - Do you supply two-way mirror glass at all?
> - What thickness do you offer it in?
> - If you can supply it, roughly what would it cost cut to 412mm x 702mm, no holes?
>
> Thanks,
> Brady

Naming Mirropane matters — "two-way mirror" gets blank looks, the Pilkington
product code doesn't.

Deliberately left out, to ask in follow-up: collection vs delivery, and edgework.
On edgework — **arrised or ground, not polished**. Polished is for frameless
mirrors where the edge is on show; yours is hidden in the frame, so it's money
for nothing. Don't go all the way down to a raw cut edge though: annealed glass
cracks start at edge chips, and arrising removes those stress risers.

## The 4mm question

If you genuinely want thinner than 6mm, there is one thread to pull: a search
snippet claims Mirrors and Glass "manufactures 4mm and 6mm mirrors and two-way
mirrors", but their own configurator offers 6mm only. Worth one email:
_"do you do the two-way in 4mm?"_ Otherwise 6mm is the market. Don't chase
thinner in glass — a 4mm beamsplitter in a large pane is also floppier and more
break-prone, and your frame already handles 6mm.

## Recommended plan

1. ~~Email the Glasgow merchants~~ — **done, drafted 2026-08-01** to all five.
   Local collection avoids shipping a big fragile pane and is probably cheapest.
2. **In parallel, buy two samples**: the Mirrors and Glass two-way sample and the
   [UK Glass Centre 6mm Mirropane sample](https://www.ukglasscentre.co.uk/product/6mm-pilkington-mirropane-one-way-mirror-sample/).
   Cheap, and lets you check reflectivity/transmission against your actual
   display before committing.
3. **Ask Mirrors and Glass two things**: Scotland delivery availability, and
   whether 4mm two-way exists.
4. **Default if local fails:** Mirrors and Glass, 6mm **untoughened**, polished
   edges, no holes.
5. Order untoughened, and be careful — annealed 6mm at mirror size is fragile
   and can't be cut or drilled after the fact. Measure twice.
